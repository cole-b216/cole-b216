### About Me

I am currently an undergraduate student at Cornell University, studying Computer Science with a focus on Game Design and AI. In the past, I have been a TA for CS1110 and MATH1110, the gateway courses to the CS path at Cornell. I am mainly interested in full stack development, game design, webdev, and accessible technology to solve real-world problems responsibly, with a user-first approach.

Reach out to me at: [`https://www.linkedin.com/in/cole-bowman`](https://www.linkedin.com/in/cole-bowman/)
